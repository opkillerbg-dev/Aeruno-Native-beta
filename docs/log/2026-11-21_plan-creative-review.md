# 2026-11-21 — Plan screen visual review

The owner reviewed the first Plan week-view and Manage-sheet exploration.

## Owner feedback

- The functional research and broad information structure are useful.
- The visual execution feels too conventional and has lost the distinctive creative quality established in AERUNO's Round 2 and Type & Depth explorations.
- The repeated progress card / date strip / checklist / settings-row pattern is too template-like.
- The Manage sheet also needs more deliberate art direction and should not read as a generic settings menu.

## Decision

The first Plan concept is **not visually approved**. Keep it as a functional/layout reference. Do not proceed to Train.

## Next

Create two materially different Plan visual concepts, each including the main week view and Manage sheet. Restore the AERUNO editorial/graphic language through deliberate scale, asymmetry and purposeful depth, while retaining usability and existing functionality. Use Depth B where it meaningfully supports weekly progress, give the selected session a stronger visual identity, and make the week rhythm feel custom to AERUNO. Avoid decoration for decoration's sake, generic glassmorphism and repeated identical cards. Show both concepts in one comparison artifact and wait for the owner to select one.

No app implementation, architecture, identity, storage, signing, native service or legacy file changes are authorized.
